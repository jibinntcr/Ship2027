Department of Ship Technology Library

The Department of Ship Technology maintains a specialized library with an extensive collection of books, international journals, rules and regulations of Classification Societies (Lloyd's Register, DNV, ABS, IRS, ClassNK), ship design project reports, and technical references in Naval Architecture, Ship Building, Marine Engineering, and Ocean Engineering.
Reference Collection

Specialized textbooks, naval architecture handbooks, and international ship design standards.

Journals & Reports

RINA transactions, SNAME periodicals, B.Tech/M.Tech thesis archives, and R&D publications.
