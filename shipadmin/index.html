EASY GUIDE FOR NON-TECHNICAL STAFF & FACULTY

How to Update the Website (Step-by-Step in Plain English)

You do NOT need any computer programming or technical skills! If you know how to type a message or attach a photo on WhatsApp/Email, you can easily update this website. And don't worry about making a mistake — anything you add can be edited or removed anytime with one click.

1. First, Meet the 4 Simple Buttons You Will Use

Every page in this control panel uses the exact same 4 buttons:

Add New ...
To Add Something New

Found at the top-right corner of each section. Click this whenever you want to add a new teacher, new photo, new brochure, or new notice.

Pencil Icon
To Edit / Fix a Mistake

Click this Pencil button next to any person or photo to fix a spelling error, change a phone number, or replace their photo.

Trash Icon
To Delete / Remove

Click this Trash button to remove an old item from the website. Don't worry — it will always ask "Are you sure?" before deleting.

Upload Photo / PDF
To Pick a Photo or PDF

Never type file names by hand! Just click this button, pick the picture or PDF from your computer/phone, and it fills in automatically.

2. How to Add or Change Anything in 3 Easy Steps
STEP 1 Click the Section on the Left Menu

Look at the dark blue menu on the left side of the screen (on a mobile phone, tap the 3-lines icon at the top-left corner). Click the section you want to change — for example, Faculty Directory or Campus Photo Gallery.

STEP 2 Fill the Simple Form & Upload File

Click + Add New (or the Pencil to edit). A simple pop-up window will open. Type the details in the boxes. If it asks for a photo or PDF, click Upload Photo or Upload PDF and choose the file from your computer or phone.

STEP 3 Click "Save & Publish"

Click the blue Save & Publish button at the bottom of the pop-up window. That's it! Your change is immediately live. You can click Open Live Website at the top-right to see how it looks.

3. Step-by-Step Guide for Every Website Section
A. Faculty, Technical Staff & Ph.D. Research Guides
Teachers, Staff & Ph.D.

Use this when a new Professor/Staff member joins, or to update Ph.D. Research Guides on the Research (`phd.php`) page.

  1. Click Faculty Directory, Technical Staff, or Ph.D. Research Guides on the left menu.
  2. Click + Add New at the top-right, or click the Pencil () next to an existing person.
  3. Full Name & Designation: Type their full name (e.g., Dr. K. Sivaprasad) and post (e.g., Professor). For Research Guides, type their research areas (one per line).
  4. Photo: Click the blue Upload Photo button and select a passport-style photo from your computer/phone.
  5. What is "Display Order"? This simply decides who is shown first on the page! Type 1 to show first, 2 for second, and so on.
  6. Click Save & Publish.
B. Uploading Placement Brochures (PDF) & Coordinators
Placement Cell

Use this to upload the latest Placement Brochure PDF or update Faculty/Student Placement Coordinators.

  1. Click Placement Brochures on the left menu and click + Add New Placement Brochure.
  2. Brochure Title: Type a clear name like Placement Brochure 2026-27.
  3. Brochure PDF File: Click the red Upload PDF (Max 25 MB) button and pick the PDF file from your computer. Wait 1–2 seconds until you see the green "Uploaded" message at the bottom-right.
  4. Cover Image: Click Upload Photo if you want to put a front-page picture on the brochure card (or leave the default cover image).
  5. To Update Coordinators: Click Placement Coordinators on the left menu. In the Role box, type either Faculty Coordinator or Student Coordinator so the website puts them under the right heading automatically.
C. Adding Photos to the Campus Photo Gallery
Photo Gallery

Use this to add photos of laboratories, campus buildings, student activities, or functions.

  1. Click Campus Photo Gallery on the left menu, then click + Add New Gallery Photo.
  2. Photo Title / Caption: Type a short line explaining what is in the picture (for example: Ship Model Testing Tank Lab or Onam Celebration).
  3. Category: Type Campus, Labs, or Events. (Tip: If you have a Google Photos album link, you can also paste that link here!)
  4. Image File: Click Upload Photo, choose the picture from your computer or phone, and click Save & Publish.
D. Scrolling News Bar & Alumni Testimonials
Homepage Notices

Use this to put important moving announcements at the top of the homepage or add alumni quotes.

  1. To Add a Scrolling Notice: Click Scrolling News Bar on the left menu → + Add New Announcement.
  2. Type your short notice in Announcement Headline (e.g., M.Tech Admissions Open for 2026 Batch).
  3. How to Show or Hide a Notice: In the Show on Website? box, type 1 to show it on the website immediately. If an announcement is old and you want to hide it without deleting, edit it and type 0!
  4. To Add an Alumni Quote: Click Alumni Testimonials → + Add New Testimonial, type the student's name, company/post, their message, and upload their photo.
E. Courses, Seminars & Department Events
Academics & Events

Use this to publish upcoming conferences, workshops, seminars, or update B.Tech / M.Tech / Ph.D details.

  1. To Add an Event: Click Events & Seminars → + Add New Event. Enter the Event Title, pick the Event Date from the calendar box, write a summary, and click Upload Photo for the event poster/photo.
  2. To Edit a Course: Click Courses & Programmes and click the Pencil () icon next to B.Tech, M.Tech, or Ph.D to update its description.
  3. Don't Worry About Long Paragraphs! Write as much detail as you like. On mobile phones, the website automatically adds a neat "Read More / Show Less" button so the page stays clean and easy to read.
F. HOD Message, Phone Numbers & Visitor Inbox
Department Info

Use this to update the Head of Department's message/photo, official phone/email, or read visitor inquiries.

  1. Changing HOD Message or Photo: Click HOD Message & Contact Info on the left menu. Edit the HOD message text or click Upload Portrait to select a new HOD photo, then click Publish Site Content Changes at the bottom-right.
  2. Changing Phone or Email: On the same page, any change to the Email or Phone Number automatically updates the top bar, bottom footer, and Contact Us page across the entire website!
  3. Reading Visitor Messages: Click Visitor Messages on the left menu to read messages sent by students or visitors from the Contact Us page.
4. Frequently Asked Questions (Common Doubts Answered)
Q1: "When adding a photo or PDF, there is a text box next to the Upload button. Do I need to type anything in that box?"

Answer: NO! You never have to type anything in that box. Just click the Upload Photo or Upload PDF button, choose the file from your computer or mobile phone, and the system will fill that box automatically for you.

Q2: "What if I make a mistake or upload the wrong photo?"

Answer: Nothing is permanent! Simply find that item in the list, click the Pencil () button to fix it, or click the red Trash () button to delete it.

Q3: "How do I check if my new photo or text is showing properly on the website?"

Answer: Click the dark blue Open Live Website button at the top-right corner of this screen. It will open the public website in a new tab so you can see your changes immediately.

Q4: "Can I update the website using my mobile phone?"

Answer: YES! Both the public website and this Admin Control Panel work smoothly on mobile phones. You can even take a photo on your phone and upload it directly to the Campus Gallery or Events page.
