Dr. Favas T K

Favas.T.K has been working as Assistant Professor at Department of Ship Technology of CUSAT since November, 2020. He worked as Lecturer in Mechanical Engineering at MES College of Engineering Kuttippuram (2004-2008) and as Ad-hoc Faculty at National Institute of Technology Calicut (2013-2018). He also served as Assistant Engineer in Kerala Water Authority (2018-2020). After completing B. Tech in Mechanical Engineering from Government Engineering College Thrissur in 2002, he completed M. Tech in Industrial Refrigeration and Cryogenic Engineering from TKM College of Engineering Kollam with Second Rank under University of Kerala in 2004. He obtained PhD in Mechanical Engineering from National Institute of Technology Calicut in 2018. He has more than 15 publications in refereed journals, national and international conferences.
