Dr. Haseena K A

Dr. Haseena K A received Bachelor of Technology in Electrical and Electronics Engineering (LBS College of Engineering, Kannur University) and Master of Technology in Power Systems (Saintgits College of Engineering, Mahatma Gandhi University). Completed Doctor of Philosophy in Dynamic Stability and Control of Renewable Energy-based Power Systems (Awarded in 2019) from the National Institute of Technology Calicut.
