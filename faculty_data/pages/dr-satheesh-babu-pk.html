Dr. Satheesh Babu P.K

Naval Architect from the 1988-92 batch of Dept of Ship Technology, CUSAT. Served Indian Navy for 22 years and retired as Commander. Experienced in various fields such as design, building and operation of ships/ submarines, quality control, research and academics.
